Questwalk Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Questwalk is a walking game for a small group of friends. It is built so that your data never leaves your phone.
What the app uses

    Steps and walking distance from Apple Health. Questwalk reads them (with your permission) to move your character along the journey. It never writes to Health.
    Your phone's motion sensor. While the app is open, it counts steps live so progress updates as you walk. Apple Health stays the source of truth.
    Notifications. Local reminders and journey events, created on your phone.

What the app does not do

    It does not send any data to a server, to the developer, or to anyone else.
    It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
    It does not share or sell data, and Health data is never used for advertising.

Where your data is stored

Your game progress and step history are stored only on your device, inside the app. Deleting the app deletes them. You can revoke Health or motion access at any time in the Settings app.
